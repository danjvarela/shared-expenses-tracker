# Last member leaving a group deletes the group

When the sole remaining member leaves a group, the group is deleted entirely — cascading its Expenses, Settlements, and PairBalances via the existing group-deletion path — rather than being left in an empty, orphaned state.

This is surprising: "Leave" reads as a self-affecting action, yet in the solo case it destroys the group and everyone's historical data. We accept the surprise because the alternative is worse. An empty group has no members to ever view or settle it, so it becomes permanent litter: unlisted in any user's group list, unreachable through the membership-gated routes, and only removable by a maintainer touching the database directly. Blocking the leave ("you can't leave, delete the group instead") forces the solo member into the Danger-zone delete dialog — a second destructive button that does the identical thing, offering no real choice and no safety, just a redundant gate.

Consequence: the removal service treats removing-oneself-as-last-member as a group deletion, so the rule holds regardless of how removal is invoked. The UI routes the solo case through the existing `?/delete` action (the Leave dialog swaps its body to the delete-group warning), keeping a single coherent exit for the solo member while the service remains the source of truth. The Danger-zone card is hidden while the group has exactly one member, so the solo member sees one Leave button rather than two destructive buttons that do the same thing. Non-last leaves remove only the membership and redirect to `/`, leaving the group and its data intact for the remaining members.
